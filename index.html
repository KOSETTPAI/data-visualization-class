Global and Thai Economic Insights: Inflation, Unemployment, and News Trends

Data sources are from World Bank and NewsAPI. Visualizations created using D3.js.

Inflation & Unemployment Rate (Thailand)

This combo chart displays the annual inflation rate (blue bars) and the total unemployment rate (red line) for Thailand from 2000 to 2023. Data is sourced from the World Bank API.

World GDP Per Capita (2022)

This World map visualizes Gross Domestic Product (GDP) per capita (current US$) for countries worldwide using data from the World Bank for the year 2022. Darker shades indicate lower GDP per capita, while brighter shades indicate higher GDP per capita. Grey areas indicate missing data.

Top Headlines Word Cloud (US)

This word cloud shows the most frequent words found in the titles of recent top news headlines from the United States, sourced via NewsAPI. Common English words (stop words) are excluded. The size of each word is proportional to its frequency in the headlines.
