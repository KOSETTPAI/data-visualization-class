Data Visualization Poster

Data sourced from World Bank and NewsAPI. Visualizations created using D3.js.

Inflation & Unemployment (Thailand)

This chart displays the annual inflation rate (consumer prices, blue bars, left axis) and the total unemployment rate (red line, right axis) for Thailand from 2000 onwards. Data is sourced from the World Bank. Hover over bars or points for specific values. Unemployment data might end earlier depending on availability (e.g., 2021).

World GDP Per Capita (2022)

This map visualizes Gross Domestic Product (GDP) per capita (current US$) for countries worldwide using data from the World Bank for the year 2022. Darker shades indicate lower GDP per capita, while brighter shades (yellow) indicate higher GDP per capita. Hover over a country to see its name and specific GDP value. Grey areas indicate missing data. Use scroll/pinch to zoom.

Top Headlines Word Cloud (US)

This word cloud shows the most frequent words found in the titles of recent top news headlines from the United States, sourced via NewsAPI. Common English words (stop words) are excluded. The size of each word is proportional to its frequency in the headlines. Requires an active internet connection and API key.
